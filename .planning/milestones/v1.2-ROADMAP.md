## Overview

This milestone ships the shared-context tracking and curation pillar for the CHESA fork of OpenWolf. It verifies already-landed P0 hygiene, re-bases the .wolf/ commit model on authored-vs-derived artifacts, closes the in-project anatomy leak with a dependency-free matcher, removes framework-specific STATUS.md in favor of a tool-agnostic resume seam, and adds continuous learnings capture plus a Git-boundary promotion gate.
