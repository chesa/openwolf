|---------|-------------|----------|---------------------|
| Scanner-only exclusion | Fixing `shouldExclude` (Q1 `respect_gitignore`, Q2 nested globs) does NOT stop anatomy leaks — the **post-write hook applies no exclusion at all**, so it re-injects excluded/gitignored/out-of-project paths. | advisory | R6: put the matcher in `shared.ts` so the hook and scanner share exclusion logic. |
